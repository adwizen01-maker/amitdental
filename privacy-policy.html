Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Amit Dental Clinic (Sector 5, Hiran Magri, Udaipur), we collect only the necessary information required to schedule appointments and provide safe dental consultations: your full name, contact phone number, preferred visit timing, and broad dental concern. We do NOT collect unnecessary sensitive financial data or excessive personal history through our website.

2. Healthcare Confidentiality

All clinical diagnostic records, dental X-rays, intraoral photographs, and medical history shared during in-clinic appointments are held in strict medical confidence in compliance with standard healthcare confidentiality norms.

3. Communications & Appointment Reminders

Contact details submitted via our booking form are used exclusively to confirm appointment schedules, provide directions to our Udaipur clinic, and deliver post-treatment follow-up instructions via call or WhatsApp. We never sell, rent, or distribute patient contact details to third-party advertisers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding patient data privacy, please contact Amit Dental Clinic directly at Riddhi Siddhi Complex, Sector 5, Hiran Magri Main Road, Udaipur, Rajasthan 313002, or call +91 98877 90414.
