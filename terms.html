Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Website Use & Medical Information

The information on this website provides general awareness of dental procedures and oral wellness practices. It does not replace individualized diagnostic assessment. Accurate dental evaluation requires an in-person physical clinical examination at our clinic in Udaipur.

2. Appointment Confirmations & Schedules

Online requests submitted via our website form or WhatsApp are requests for booking. Our clinic reception confirms the exact date and appointment timing directly with you. If you need to reschedule, we kindly appreciate early notice so urgent emergency patients can be accommodated.

3. Clinical Treatment Consent

Before initiating any dental procedure (such as root canals, crowns, aligners, or extractions), Dr. Amit Sharma and the clinical team provide clear explanations of the procedure, expected duration, and financial transparency. Treatment is undertaken only with your informed consent.
